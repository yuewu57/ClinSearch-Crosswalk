# Licensing and public-release review

## Release status

The release-preparation branch proposes PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.
Do not merge/publish it until the relevant rights-holders have approved the change.
Neither repository control nor a copyright notice establishes sole ownership.
The existing contributor attribution is retained; no transfer to QueSquared or
another company is asserted by this change.

## Public wording

Source available under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.
Uses outside its permitted purposes require a separate licence from the relevant
rights-holder. This is source-available software, not OSI-approved open source.

Use the unmodified official licence text in LICENSE. Its organisational permission
is broader than a blanket "no commercial context" rule: the listed charitable,
educational, public research, public safety/health, environmental and government
organisations are expressly permitted regardless of funding source or obligations
resulting from funding. Do not replace this with an inaccurate "all research is
free / every company use is banned" summary. Ask the project maintainers about
separate licensing for uses outside the licence's permitted purposes.

## Checks before publication

1. Confirm ownership and authority for all copyrightable contributions, including
   any university, student, employee, contractor or collaborator contributions.
2. Identify third-party code, datasets, terminology and images. Preserve their
   applicable licences and notices; the project licence does not replace them.
3. Review whether any version was actually supplied under Apache-2.0. A new
   licence does not revoke an earlier valid grant. A clean Git snapshot does not
   revoke those rights either.
4. Review all tracked files, examples and tests for confidential material,
   credentials and redistribution permissions. A clean archive excludes .git
   history, not sensitive files still present in the selected tree.
5. Confirm CITATION.cff authors, release date and the exact paper/software release.
6. Review hosting terms separately. Streamlit Community Cloud terms section 6.2
   grants Snowflake separate, broad rights to submitted content. A PolyForm
   notice does not override that hosting agreement. Obtain appropriate advice
   before accepting terms where commercial-use control is important.

The paper, code, third-party data and brand assets need not share a licence.
No corporate or institutional logo is required to run the public interface.
